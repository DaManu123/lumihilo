How to play

Simple rules, endless possibilities

Learn it in 5 seconds. Tap, rotate and watch the light spread.

01

Find the source

The glowing core sends energy looking for a path through the whole circuit.

02

Rotate the pieces

One tap turns a piece 90°. If you overshoot, use Undo to go back instantly.

03

Complete the circuit

When every piece lights up, the circuit is complete and the puzzle ends with a satisfying glow.
